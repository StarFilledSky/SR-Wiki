


### What is Slope Surfing
Slope Surfing is a tech where you jump before hitting a slope. putting you into a jump state that lets you avoid the downsides of touching the ground.

 After jumping, a 0.25 second jump state starts where the player won't connect to the floor. It keeps you in the air state and pushes you up the slope and allows you avoid ground friction while not changing the direction of your velocity. We can benifit from this in multiple ways.  

- we can glide up a slope with only air friction being applied keeping more speed than if we walked or slid over it.
- We can fall lower into a swing when approaching rising terrain and gain more map progress and speed than we would taking a more shallow swing over the rising terrain.
